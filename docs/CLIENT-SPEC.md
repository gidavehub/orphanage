# The Orphanage Project: client specification (source of truth)

The approved Terms of Reference, condensed without loss. Section numbers match the original document. The site is branded **Haven** (see `HANDOFF.md`). Where this spec and the owner's direction (bottom of this file) differ, the owner's direction wins.

Project details:
- **Platform:** a combined non-profit information, support, donation, resource and administration platform.
- **Development period:** 7 September to 2 November 2026.
- **Budget:** GMD 50,000, plus GMD 10,000 a year for support after the 30-day warranty.

## 1. Positioning
- **Focus:** protecting, assisting and supporting vulnerable children, families and other verifiable charitable organisations, through education, healthcare, psychosocial assistance, legal support and community-based interventions.
- **Tagline:** "Restoring Hope. Protecting Childhood. Building Futures." Alternative: "Every Child Deserves Safety, Care and Opportunity."
- **What the platform does:**
  - Explains the mission and programmes.
  - Takes cash and in-kind donations.
  - Lets people request support or refer a vulnerable child.
  - Connects beneficiaries with medical, psychological and legal assistance.
  - Shares reports, policies and resources.
  - Publishes impact stories and updates.
  - Gives staff a secure admin dashboard.

## 2. Structure
- **Main navigation:** Home, About Us, Our Programmes, Get Support, Resources, Our Impact, Get Involved, Donate, Contact.
- **Additional links:** Child Safeguarding Policy, Privacy Policy, Terms of Use, Report a Concern, Request a Report, Administrator Login.

## 4. About Us
- **Who we are:** a non-profit protecting vulnerable children and supporting families in very difficult circumstances. It works with communities, professionals, donors and partners, connecting families to education, healthcare, counselling, economic inclusion and legal assistance. Its approach strengthens the environment around the child through direct assistance, family support, referrals, advocacy, community education and partnerships.
- **Vision:** "Societies that cultivate safe, dignified, and economically inclusive environments, empowering every child to thrive and reach their full potential."
- **Mission:** "To protect vulnerable children, enhance their economic prosperity, and strengthen families by connecting them with education, healthcare, psychosocial support, legal assistance and sustainable community-based opportunities."
- **Values:** Child-centred, Compassion, Integrity, Inclusion, Accountability (transparent about work, financials and impact), Collaboration, Confidentiality.
- **Testimonials:** from beneficiaries, donors, volunteers, partners and professionals, published only with consent.

## 6. Get Support
- **Heading:** "You Do Not Have to Face It Alone". It is for children, parents, caregivers, teachers, community members and professionals.
- **Review policy:** every request is reviewed against policy, resources and safeguarding procedures. Submitting a request does not guarantee support.
- **Categories:**
  - child-protection concern
  - education or school fees
  - medical
  - psychological or counselling
  - legal
  - food or essential supplies
  - family support
  - other
- **Form fields:**
  - applicant's full name
  - relationship to the child
  - phone
  - email
  - location or country
  - preferred contact method
  - type of support
  - brief explanation
  - urgency
  - whether immediate danger is involved
  - consent to be contacted
  - privacy acknowledgement
- **Sensitive documents:** ask for them only with secure storage and access control.
- **Verification:** because of fraud, all information is checked and verified.

## 7. Report a Child-Protection Concern
This form is separate from the contact form.
- **Notice:** "If a child is in immediate danger, contact the appropriate emergency, law-enforcement or child-protection authority immediately. Do not wait for a response from this website."
- **Fields:**
  - reporter's name, or anonymous
  - contact details
  - relationship to the child
  - child's approximate age
  - location
  - nature of the concern
  - whether the child is in danger now
  - whether authorities have been contacted
  - safe method and time for follow-up
  - consent and privacy statement
- **Workflow:**
  1. The concern is submitted securely.
  2. Authorised safeguarding personnel get an alert.
  3. The concern is reviewed and given a risk level.
  4. Immediate-risk cases are escalated.
  5. Services or authorities are contacted.
  6. Actions and follow-ups are recorded.
  7. Access is restricted to approved personnel only. Ordinary admins cannot see these cases.

## 8. Donations
- **Cash:** one-time, monthly, quarterly or annual. Programme-specific or general.
- **In kind:**
  - school materials
  - clothing
  - bedding
  - food supplies
  - medical supplies
  - computers or learning devices
  - furniture
  - professional services
  - other approved items
- **Page copy:** "Give Hope Create Opportunity: Your contribution will help a vulnerable child return to school, gain economic empowerment, live with dignity, access medical care, receive counselling and obtain essential protection and family support."
- **Form fields:**
  - donor's full name
  - individual or organisation
  - email
  - phone
  - country
  - donation type
  - amount or estimated in-kind value
  - frequency
  - preferred programme
  - anonymous-recognition option
  - comments
  - consent to updates
- **Workflow:**
  1. The donor chooses cash or in kind.
  2. Cash donors go to the approved payment method.
  3. In-kind donors describe the items.
  4. The admin is notified.
  5. The donation is reviewed and acknowledged.
  6. A receipt is issued.
  7. The status is recorded in the dashboard.
  8. The donor may receive approved impact updates.
- **Card data:** never store full card details.

## 9. Resources & Reports
- **Categories:**
  - child safeguarding
  - child rights and human rights
  - parenting and caregiving
  - mental health and wellbeing
  - education
  - medical information
  - legal information
  - organisational policies
  - annual and programme reports
  - research and publications
- **Report card fields:** title, cover image, publication date, category, short description, executive summary or preview, and a "Request Full Report" button.
- **Request flow:**
  1. The visitor selects a report.
  2. The visitor submits name, organisation, email, country and reason.
  3. The admin is notified.
  4. The admin approves or declines.
  5. An approved report is emailed.
  6. The status is recorded.
- **Access:** non-sensitive public reports can be downloaded immediately. Controlled distribution applies only to sensitive reports.

## 12. Admin dashboard
- **Main view** shows:
  - new support requests
  - pending child-protection concerns
  - donation enquiries
  - in-kind offers
  - counselling appointments
  - resource requests
  - volunteer applications
  - partnership enquiries
  - published resources
  - recent activity
- **Content:** pages, programme information, news and updates, testimonials, impact statistics, images and media, FAQs.
- **Support requests:** view, assign a case reference, categorise, assign an officer, update status, add internal notes, record referrals, close or archive.
- **Donations:** record cash and in-kind donations, set frequency, record acknowledgement, track programme allocation, export basic reports.
- **Counselling:** review requests, assign a counsellor, schedule, generate or attach a Zoom link, send notifications, record attendance, keep limited follow-up notes.
- **Resources:** upload, add previews, set public or controlled access, review requests, approve email delivery, record activity.
- **Volunteers and partners:** review, categorise skills or interests, update status, assign follow-up, export contacts.
- **Roles and access:**

  | Role | Access |
  |---|---|
  | Super Administrator | Everything |
  | Content Manager | Pages, news, resources and media |
  | Programme Officer | Support requests and programme records |
  | Safeguarding Officer | Restricted child-protection cases |
  | Finance or Donation Officer | Donations and financial records |
  | Counsellor | Only their assigned counselling appointments |
  | Report Manager | Resource and report requests |
  | Viewer or Auditor | Read-only access to approved areas |

  Sensitive data is restricted by role.

## 13. Status workflows
- **Support request:** New → Under review → More information required → Assigned → Referred → Approved → Support in progress → Completed → Declined → Closed
- **Donation:** Pledged → Pending → Received → Acknowledged → Allocated → Completed → Cancelled
- **Resource request:** New → Under review → Approved → Sent → Declined → Closed
- **Volunteer:** Submitted → Screening → Interview → Approved → Onboarding → Active → Inactive → Declined

## 14. Email notifications
Automated emails for:
- support-request confirmation
- child-protection alert (to authorised staff)
- donation acknowledgement
- in-kind follow-up
- counselling confirmation
- Zoom reminder
- resource-request confirmation
- approved report delivery
- volunteer acknowledgement
- partnership acknowledgement
- contact confirmation

Emails about sensitive cases never put confidential detail in the subject line.

## 15. Safeguarding & privacy (mandatory)
- Written parent or guardian consent before publishing children's images or stories.
- No full names or precise locations of children.
- Never publish case files or applications.
- Restrict sensitive records by role.
- Strong admin passwords, with multi-factor authentication where possible.
- An admin activity log.
- HTTPS.
- Minimal data collection.
- Defined retention periods.
- A process to correct or delete information.
- Secure backups.
- Professional review of policies.
- Automated tools never "diagnose".

## Owner's direction (2026-10-09). This overrides the spec where they differ.
- **Build everything, comprehensively**, in the current Haven style and design. Don't copy the spec's structure literally: its author lacked the full UI/UX picture. Use it for requirements, and design the experience properly.
- **Donation page:** a dedicated page with a shareable link, as well as the landing donate section. Donors choose a cause or programme and see exactly where the money goes and how it will be spent, in a friendly UX.
- **Payments:** Stripe, through Next.js route handlers on Vercel. Wire everything up now, and the keys come later. The site must work gracefully without keys.
- **Backend:** Firebase (Auth, Firestore, Storage) for admin, authentication and data. Credentials come later, so build against env vars, with a local fallback so the site runs and can be verified now.
- **Admin:**
  - Must cover blog posting, monthly AI-drafted blog automation with human review, posting updates, and editing landing-page content and key information.
  - **Must not** be the usual sidebar with a pile of toggles in the middle. It must feel easy, warm and simple for non-technical staff.
  - Fully mobile-friendly.
- **Everything beautiful:** animations, polish, the same brand system as the landing page.
- **Phased delivery is fine.**
